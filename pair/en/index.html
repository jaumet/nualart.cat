pAIr brings this idea to real challenges faced by freelancers and businesses. You bring business knowledge; Jaume brings facilitation, digital experience and technical judgement. AI contributes knowledge, alternatives and production.

There is no fixed hierarchy. One person may know the discipline, the other the technology, and AI may know more than either in one part of the problem. Responsibility for the result remains human.

Building means more than making it work

02 / TECHNICAL JUDGEMENT

What can fail when AI programs what nobody understands?

An app can work
and still be dangerous.

AI lets people build without knowing code, servers or databases. It can also leave invisible errors that only surface when real users, data or attackers arrive.

There is no rigorous figure for “all apps made by non-programmers”. The closest evidence is this: in a study of 200 real, deployed vibe-coded applications, 90% contained at least one vulnerability; over three quarters were high severity or critical.

4 of 5

tasks in a user study produced less secure solutions with an AI assistant.

01

Permissions that do not protect

A screen may look private while the server allows access to or changes in somebody else’s data.

02

Exposed secrets

API keys, passwords or personal data may end up in the browser, repository or public logs.

03

Inputs turned into attacks

Unvalidated forms can open the door to injection, cross-site scripting or malicious content.

04

Unsafe dependencies

AI may suggest packages that do not exist, are obsolete or vulnerable, turning installation into an entry point.

05

No plan for the day after

Without backups, logs, updates and rollback, a working demonstration is not yet a maintainable product.

pAIr

The answer is not to stop building.

It is to combine AI’s power with technical judgement: understand the architecture, inspect the code, test the limits and take responsibility before publishing.

Sources and scope

  1. Deng et al. (2026), Understanding the (In)Security of Vibe-Coded Applications: 200 deployed apps, 1,471 vulnerabilities, 90% with at least one.
  2. Veracode (2025), GenAI Code Security Report: over 100 models; 45% of samples failed security tests. This is not a percentage of complete apps.
  3. Perry et al. (2023), Do Users Write More Insecure Code with AI Assistants?: controlled study with 47 participants and five security tasks.

Necessary questions

Before doing pAIr

Can I do pAIr without hiring nualart.cat?+

Yes. pAIr is an open practice: meet another person, choose a real project and work with AI as your main tool. The paid service is professional support, not the right to practise pAIr.

What do I hire in a pAIr Session?+

Preparation, facilitation, technical judgement and shared working time. We agree the intention, duration and possible result beforehand. Pure execution work is separated.

Is AI a tool or a third intelligence?+

Both: the main tool and a participating intelligence that can know more, reason, propose and produce. This does not remove human responsibility for goals, verification and consequences.

Is it only for programming?+

No. It originates in pair programming, but the practice can research, write, design, develop a campaign, review a product or transform a working process.

Do I need to know AI or programming?+

Not to begin: you need to know your context or have a real question. But if the result is software to be published, somebody must understand its architecture, code, servers and security.

When does it become freelance work?+

When production no longer needs shared conversation and decisions, but hours of specialised execution. We separate it from the session and quote it independently.
